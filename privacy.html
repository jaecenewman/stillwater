Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 · Application: Stillwater Personal Command Center

Stillwater (“we,” “our,” or “the app”) is a personal productivity and calm task-organization web application. Your privacy is fundamental to Stillwater’s design. This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, how it is used and stored, and how you maintain full control over your data.

1. Information We Access & Collect

When you choose to sign in and connect optional integrations, Stillwater accesses only the minimum data needed to display and organize your personal workspace:

  • Google Account Profile (Firebase Authentication): Your name, email address, and profile photo used solely to authenticate your account and isolate your private workspace.
  • Google Tasks (tasks.readonly): Read-only access to your open Google Tasks titles and notes so you can import them into your Stillwater task lists.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.readonly): Read-only access to your primary calendar events for the current day so you can view your schedule inside the “Calendar & Blocks” tab.
  • User-Created Workspace Data: Tasks, structured fields (task title, details, room/context, location), focus blocks, and companion preferences you create inside Stillwater.
  • Optional Gemini AI Key: If you provide your own Google AI Studio / Gemini API key, it is stored locally in your browser’s localStorage and sent directly from your browser to Google’s Generative Language API solely to parse your quick-capture text into structured task fields.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to provide Stillwater’s core user-facing features:

  • Displaying your tasks, schedule events, and focus blocks inside your personal Stillwater interface.
  • Syncing your Stillwater tasks and settings across your devices via Google Firebase Firestore.
  • Organizing natural-language notes into structured task fields when you trigger Smart Sort.
Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
Stillwater’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We never sell your Google user data, never use it for advertising, and never share it with third-party data brokers.

3. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • Local & Cloud Sync: Your workspace items are stored locally in your browser and, when signed in, synced securely over HTTPS to your private user document in Google Firebase Firestore (Project: stillwater-a-quiet-plae), protected by Firebase Authentication security rules so only your authenticated account can read or write your records.
  • OAuth Access Tokens: Temporary Google OAuth tokens for Tasks and Calendar are held only in your browser’s active sessionStorage and are cleared when you sign out or close your browser session.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete individual tasks, clear completed items, or clear your local browser storage at any time directly inside Stillwater. To request full deletion of your cloud-synced Firestore record, contact us at the email below.

4. Revoking Access

You may revoke Stillwater’s access to your Google Account, Google Tasks, and Google Calendar at any time via your Google Account Third-Party Connections page.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact the developer at jaece.newman@gmail.com.
