welcome to stillwater · quick guide
1. quick capture: tap mic or type multiple thoughts at once. stillwater splits clauses and routes them into today, errands, ideas, or @waiting on automatically.
2. calm submenus: tap any category pill across the middle to filter your view, or switch between task list, big 3 focus, calendar & blocks, and tools & nfc.
3. warm reading lamp: tap lamp in the top bar anytime for a non-backlit e-ink paper feel with adjustable warmth and dimmer sliders.
4. pip & cross-device sync: finishing tasks earns treats for pip. tap sign in to sync in the top-right to keep your phone and computer synced via google.
